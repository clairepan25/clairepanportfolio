How I Work

My design process

Every project starts with listening. Understanding the brief, the audience, and the story we're trying to tell — before a single design decision is made.

01
Research

Studying the client's brand identity, desired look and feel, and target audience. Understanding the why before touching the how.

02
Design

Sketching directions and crafting 2–3 distinct design options for the client to explore — each with a clear visual personality and rationale.

03
Deliver

Refining with feedback until it's exactly right. Final, production-ready files delivered clean and on time — every time.

UX Case Study · In Progress

Mobile App Redesign

Improving discoverability and reducing checkout friction for a local food delivery app.

RoleUX Designer (Solo)
ToolsFigma, Maze, FigJam
DurationOngoing
StatusWireframing Phase
The Problem

Users of the existing app struggled to find new restaurants and frequently abandoned the checkout flow due to confusing pricing and too many steps. Drop-off rate at checkout was above 60%.

My Process
01
Research
5 user interviews + competitive analysis of Grab Food, FoodPanda, and Deliveroo.
02
Define
Affinity mapping, user personas, and journey maps to identify pain points.
03
Design
Low-fi sketches → wireframes in Figma → interactive prototype for testing.
Key Insights

Users want to browse by mood or occasion, not just cuisine type. The existing category system was too generic. A visual-first browsing experience with contextual filters increased task completion in prototype testing by 40%.

UX Case Study · Completed

Portfolio Website UX

Applying UX thinking to my own digital presence — from research to launch.

RoleUX + Visual Design
ToolsFigma, Webflow, Hotjar
Duration4 weeks
StatusLaunched
The Challenge

My previous portfolio was a simple template that didn't communicate my creative range or personality. It wasn't converting visitors into inquiries — people would browse but not reach out.

My Process
01
Audit
Heatmap analysis and 3 user interviews with potential clients and recruiters.
02
Redesign
New IA, warm earthy visual identity, and clear conversion paths throughout.
03
Validate
A/B tested two hero variants. Contact form submissions increased by 3×.
Before / After
Outcome

The redesigned portfolio reduced bounce rate by 28% and tripled contact form submissions in the first month after launch. The warm earthy palette and editorial typography helped differentiate from typical designer portfolios.
